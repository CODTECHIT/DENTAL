## 12. Internal Linking Strategy

### Site architecture (hub & spoke)
```
                        / (Homepage — HUB: Dentist in Tukkuguda)
                       /|\
         ┌─────────────┼─────────────────┐
    /services      /team,/about     /blog (content hub)
        │              │                │
  /services/{16}   doctor pages    100+ articles
        │                            │
  /dentist-in-{18 areas}  ◄──────────┘  (area pages link back to services)
```

### Link rules
1. **Every service page** links to: homepage, 3–4 related service pages ("Related treatments"), the relevant specialist (e.g., implant page → /team#dr-akhil), and 2–3 matching blog posts.
2. **Every blog post** links to: the relevant service page (main CTA), 2 related posts, and the homepage. Never orphan a post.
3. **Every area page** (`/dentist-in-{area}`) links to: homepage, top 4 services, and 1 relevant post. Homepage links to every area page (footer "Dentist Near You" block).
4. **Anchors:** use descriptive, keyword-rich anchors: "dental implants in Tukkuguda", "cost of root canal treatment", "Invisalign clear aligners". Limit exact-match anchors to ~10–15% — vary with "here", "our implant specialists", brand names.
5. **Breadcrumbs** on all inner pages (Home → Services → Root Canal Treatment) — strengthens crawl depth and shows in SERPs.
6. **Depth:** every important page reachable within 3 clicks. Add HTML sitemap in footer for users and a linked sitemap.xml.
7. **Homepage outbound:** services (16), team, about, gallery, contact, 4–6 area pages, FAQ anchor. Keep links purposeful, not over-stuffed.
8. **Dead-end audit monthly:** use Screaming Frog (free up to 500 URLs) — fix orphans and 404s.

### Anti-duplicate rules

- Each page needs ≥1 unique element: local landmark reference, commute time, local event/school/industry mention (e.g., Adibatla SEZ, Shamshabad airport proximity).
